Companion phone app for the jednadvacet phone GSM↔SIP gateway. It connects to
your own gateway box over a private encrypted network and lets you make and
receive calls and SMS from your SIM card anywhere.

Setup is a single QR scan from the gateway's control panel — no accounts.

This is a fork of Linphone (GPLv3) by Belledonne Communications, rebranded and
adapted for the jednadvacet ecosystem. Source:
https://github.com/twentyone-cz/jednadvacet-phone-android

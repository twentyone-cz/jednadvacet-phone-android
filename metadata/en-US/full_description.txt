Phone21 is the phone app for your own Phone21 miniserver. It connects to the
miniserver over a private encrypted network and lets you call and text from
your own number anywhere, as if you were at home.

Setup is a single QR scan from the miniserver's control panel — no accounts to
create.

Phone21 is a fork of Linphone (GPLv3) by Belledonne Communications, rebranded
and adapted for Phone21. Source:
https://github.com/twentyone-cz/phone21-android
